# Stage-2 task brief: observation-innovation trigger

## Pre-launch gate

1. **Mechanism question:** Can a declared observation innovation make the
   inspection/stop trigger respond before the model-only committee, while FEM
   mechanism metrics remain an audit-only post-hoc join?
2. **Claim changed by success or failure:** Success can support only an
   interface-level claim that observation innovation adds transition signal on
   this synthetic trajectory. Failure would show the available proxy channels
   do not repair the late model-only trigger. Neither outcome validates road
   early warning.
3. **Cheapest diagnostic:** Reuse the 18 frozen matched predictions, the single
   eta0 FEM trajectory, Agent1's frozen observation contract, and Agent3's input
   contract. No new model training or FEM solve.
4. **Minimal output asset:** Versioned packet and trigger specifications, one
   policy-comparison CSV, one trajectory CSV/figure, and a decision note.
5. **Checklist entry:** Close the local attempt ledger and preserve hashes in a
   package manifest; hand the accepted interface and quarantined numerical
   result back to the road-observation/forecast agents.

## Frozen protocol

- Calibration steps: c77-c79 only.
- Warning/hard thresholds: robust z=3/5 inherited from stage 1; no sweep.
- c80 onward: evaluation only.
- Runtime inputs: model uncertainty/self-drift, declared observation
  innovation, traffic/environment OOD, data quality, overdue inspection, and
  maintenance.
- FEM IoU/error: `audit_only_*`, joined after decisions.
- Crack geometry: synthetic visibility/geometry operator from FEM damage.
- FWD: unavailable and masked; no fabricated basin.
- Strain localization: oracle-derived raw-energy localization proxy, explicitly
  not a measured strain channel.
- The diagnostic may report simulation-step lead/lag only. It has no ESAL,
  axle, or calendar-time interpretation.
