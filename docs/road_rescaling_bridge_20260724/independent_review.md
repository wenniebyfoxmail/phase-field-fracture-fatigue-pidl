# Independent Rescaling Review

The independent review accepted a dimensionless similarity study and rejected
direct toy-to-road interpretation.

Adopted points:

- Preserve `ell/L`, `a0/L`, layer and load-patch ratios, `h/ell`, Poisson ratio,
  material contrasts, normalized load, `alpha_T/(G_c/ell)`, load ratio, model
  family, split, residual stiffness, and plane condition.
- Treat fatigue history and `alpha_T` as energy densities. Normalize both by
  `G_c/ell`.
- Treat FWD inversion, fracture length scale, and fatigue threshold as
  non-identifiable from a single observation type.
- Use an exact-dimensionless positive control before a layered-road anchor.
- Keep cycle-to-traffic mapping unavailable until load blocks, environment, and
  fatigue calibration exist.

Rejected shortcuts:

- coordinate-only scaling;
- treating one PIDL cycle as one vehicle, ESAL, day, or year;
- treating 2D plane strain as a moving-wheel 3D road model;
- treating surface crack imagery as direct observation of hidden fatigue or
  energetic fields;
- claiming that three numerical trajectories establish road generalisation.
