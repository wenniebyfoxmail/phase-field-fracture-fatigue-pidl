## Goal and mechanism

Original programme: trustworthy observations of cracks, then useful prediction of their evolution. Retain the distinction between real roads, controlled laboratory material, published observation targets and latent physical damage.

An observation can be written as y=O(x,c)+e: underlying state x, capture/annotation conditions c, and observation error e. Identification compresses y into a point geometry or uncertain state. The portfolio must determine which limitation binds:

1. The feature representation fails under source change.
2. The label/target convention changes although a crack-like feature remains observable.
3. The visible state omits history/response needed for evolution.
4. Measurement uncertainty is not identifiable from the available reference chain.

Changing a backbone addresses hypothesis1 at most. It does not automatically resolve hypotheses2–4. Requesting another dataset does not itself discriminate any of them.
