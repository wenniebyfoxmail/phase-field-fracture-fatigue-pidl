## What makes a step actionable

Check only whether the active step identifies an uncertainty, a smallest
action with existing inputs, an observable deliverable, and an advance or
return/redirect condition. Reuse finished steps. Do not require a new number,
new review, perfect evidence or a numerical target where none is justified.
Clearly marked external dependencies and paused/completed work are legitimate
states; do not invent activity to make every HQ appear busy.

## Scope

Inspected five portfolio/scientific HQs, plus six principal active or recently
executing direction owners. Also screened the saved LTPP, residual-solver and
FPRM status for clarity. Historic completed writing, source-reading and audit
chats were not revived. App titles are retained verbatim in the receipt file.

Three recurring issues were observed:

1. Acquisition/full-text activities did not state the scientific choice they
   enabled. Convert waiting to a dependency, not the whole HQ purpose.
2. A choice or execution had completed while its old label remained active.
   Move to the actual next decision rather than repeat source/method selection.
3. A next direction was clear but did not yet specify inputs, output or a branch.
   Write a small preparation card, not a fresh run or an additional review.

Each original owner was asked to refine its own record and existing Obsidian
note/Roadmap, avoiding concurrent edits to scientific protocols. Proposals are
not adopted steps until the owner receipt and written record are checked.
