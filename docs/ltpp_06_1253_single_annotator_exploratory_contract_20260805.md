# LTPP 06-1253 single-annotator exploratory contract

## Purpose

This branch permits progress with the locked primary annotation while an
independent secondary pass is unavailable. It is an evidence downgrade, not a
replacement for the independent vectorization and adjudication protocol.

## Required inputs

1. The existing observation-only geometry-climate preflight must have no
   blockers other than missing blind pairing and missing adjudicated geometry.
2. All nine primary maps must be locked under `line_area_v2`.
3. The primary file hashes and line/polygon totals must be frozen in a manifest.
4. The authorized forcing library remains limited to low-temperature exposure,
   temperature-variation rate, and precipitation rate.

## Authorized work

- After all primary maps are locked and hashed, the modelling process may read
  the sealed blind-ID/date mapping solely to restore temporal order. The mapping
  and all derived chronological outputs must remain unavailable to any future
  secondary annotator.
- Fit a continuous observation-field adapter to the single-annotator geometry.
- Freeze that field before any equation-term selection.
- Report selection frequencies across seeds, time windows, spatial sections,
  and weak-test perturbations as exploratory results.
- Compare against single-shot and joint-training baselines at the same
  exploratory evidence grade.

## Prohibited claims

- The primary geometry is not ground truth or an adjudicated reference.
- Selection results are not confirmatory real-road support recovery.
- No annotation-accuracy, cross-annotator-reliability, traffic, moisture,
  mechanics, phase-field-teacher, natural crack morphology, exact crack width,
  centimetre-scale true tip, or cross-road generalization claim is permitted.

## Future upgrade

The exploratory qualifier may be removed only after a different annotator
locks all nine secondary maps, blind line/area pairing is run, every mismatch is
adjudicated, and a hashed adjudicated geometry manifest passes the frozen gate.
That annotator must not see the date mapping, primary files, or exploratory
model outputs.
