# External review: LTPP spatial-registration audit v1

## Initial decision

ChatGPT Pro returned `REVISE_V1_BEFORE_PREFLIGHT` before any new registration
audit result was computed.

The reviewer accepted the separable affine audit and the engineering thresholds
in principle, but required the following minimum changes:

1. Treat the 15 intersections as a dependent grid generated by five held-out x
   lines and three held-out y lines, not as 15 independent samples.
2. Freeze deskew/detection provenance, line-centre extraction, missing-line
   behavior, and the tie-break.
3. Freeze the p95 implementation.
4. Describe `0.05/0.10/0.20 m` as a-priori engineering qualification gates,
   not as statistically derived from annotation disagreement.
5. Remove or fully specify the proposed perturbation analysis; it must not gate
   qualification because three y calibration lines provide only one residual
   degree of freedom.
6. Preserve the all-dates-pass rule and prohibit date deletion or transform
   rescue.

## Local decision

All six changes were adopted. Most importantly, the 500-draw perturbation was
removed completely from v1. The revised design reuses the frozen acquisition
receipt without rerunning the detector and uniquely fixes line candidates,
tolerance, tie-break, affine fit, quantile convention, thresholds, statuses, and
termination behavior.

The reviewer stated that after these changes the work could enter control-point
freeze, script/unit-test construction, and one `06-1253` preflight. This does
not authorize 2-D model training, failed-date deletion, or registration rescue.

The exact revised design was then resubmitted without results. ChatGPT Pro
returned `APPROVE_REVISED_V1_PREFLIGHT_ONLY`.
