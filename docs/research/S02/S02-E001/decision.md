# Decision

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Decision:** `NO_GO_NEURAL_TRAINING__QUALIFY_EVENTS_AND_BASELINES_FIRST`  
**Execution status:** no FEM or PIDL/GNO/neural/statistical training launched

## Outcome

The two-stage framework is retained as the correct research decomposition:
transition warning is evaluated separately from regime-conditioned propagation,
and autonomous rollout is separated from post-observation assimilation.

Training is not scientifically justified under the current S02-E001 data
release.

## Why the gate fails

1. P0 and T3 do not resolve to unique trajectory/state manifests in the current
   evidence and are excluded.
2. The four factorial FEM trajectories share geometry, mesh, material and
   Umax=0.12. They support a narrow synthetic LOTO diagnostic, not a general
   transition predictor.
3. The three cross-Umax Hard5 trajectories are already-seen development paths
   from the same physical family.
4. One c87 transition and the prior single-trajectory temporal study cannot
   establish transition generalization; its autonomous c87/c89 rollout failed.
5. The six LTPP sections contain 37 registered states and 31 leakage-safe
   geometry-climate transitions, but no frozen road regime-transition event
   ontology and no complete observable join across load, environment,
   mechanics and maintenance.
6. CUSUM and discrete-time hazard warning controls have not been evaluated;
   matched Markov warning probabilities and calibration are incomplete.

## Allowed claims

- The two-stage warning/propagation/assimilation estimand is preregistered.
- Complete-trajectory/asset holdout and causal leakage fences are specified.
- Same-regime short-horizon FEM propagation can outperform persistence for
  selected channels; the two-frame secant is the strongest current transparent
  short-horizon synthetic control.
- Existing temporal neural models did not autonomously generate the held-out
  c87/c89 transition on the single reused trajectory.
- Correct c87 state assimilation can restore conditional c89 continuation,
  supporting a missing-state-information hypothesis.
- Persistence remains the honest LTPP road-geometry point control on the
  current six-section benchmark.

## Blocked claims

- a general transition-warning predictor;
- calibrated h1-h3 transition probability;
- autonomous fracture-transition prediction across trajectories;
- road deployment or real-road mechanism forecasting;
- neural switching/GNO/TCN/Transformer superiority;
- physical/PINO validity from GNO-data or hidden-field imitation;
- use of P0/T3 as qualified trajectories before unique identity resolution;
- treating confirmation, terminal cycle, target interval forcing or future
  observations as causal inputs;
- treating seeds, nodes, cells, windows or six final transitions as independent
  road-scale evidence.

## Smallest next gate

1. Resolve or exclude P0/T3 using unique manifests and state/event receipts.
2. Freeze one Tier-B road-observable event ontology on development assets.
3. Produce a complete asset-time join/exclusion table for geometry, load,
   environment, mechanics, maintenance and censoring.
4. Evaluate persistence, secant, CUSUM, discrete-time hazard and matched Markov
   in that order under complete-trajectory holdout.
5. Populate warning lead/FPR/PR/Brier and the separate rollout/assimilation
   figure suite.

Only a passed, independently reviewed baseline package may reopen a named
neural run. Reopening still requires separate owner authorization and a frozen
producer receipt.
