# Data qualification and leakage audit

**Audit date:** 2026-08-27  
**Execution:** read-only; no FEM, PIDL, GNO or statistical training launched

## Verdict

`FAIL_NEURAL_ENTRY_GATE__BASELINE_AND_DATA_QUALIFICATION_FIRST`

The proposed two-stage estimand is scientifically coherent, but the current
evidence does not justify neural training for a general transition-aware
fracture forecaster.

| Future forcing | LTPP interval files may include target-inclusive climate summaries | Use only forcing actually available at issue time, or label a separate scenario forecast |

| Reset/maintenance crossing | Real-road maintenance joins remain incomplete | Exclude ambiguous intervals; model maintenance as an observed action/reset only after join |
| Alias collision | P0 and T3 have multiple meanings in project documents | Require unique manifest/source/state resolution before inclusion |
| Post-result rescue | Several prior negative gates are frozen | No threshold, predicate, ROI, mask or architecture tuning on seen holdouts |

  cycle and window-crossing splits.

  c122/c83/c59; all are development-only and already seen.

  transitions, not a qualified regime-transition warning label.
- Real-road coverage audit: zero possessed/downloadable routes pass the full
  road-observable trajectory gate.

## Consequence

Offline transparent-control implementation is scientifically justified after
P0/T3 exclusion and an explicit event amendment. Neural switching/GNO/TCN/
Transformer training is not justified by S02-E001 v1 and remains unauthorized.
