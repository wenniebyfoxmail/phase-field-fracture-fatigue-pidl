## Concrete next solution

Protect the existing E003 proposals and scores. Future corrections should be
incremental candidates assessed against originals, rather than mandatory
replacements of every box. Use local spatial features to compare original and
corrected geometry; the present global pooled classification features did not
provide effective box corrections in this experiment.

The next design must first freeze candidate preservation and train-only
out-of-sample qualification of any original-versus-correction selector, then
pass independent review. Its primary remains actual recall at the same false-
positive budget. The proposal is in next_safe_refinement_proposal.md; it is
untested and no additional arm or fit was executed under E005.
