# S01-E002 result — 2026-10-11

## Decision

S01-E002 is **negative for its frozen conjunctive primary claim**. The tiled
route improved macro-location recall at one false positive per image from
0.113710 to 0.183602, a delta of +0.069892, which does not reach the frozen
+0.10 primary gate. Candidate-set oracle recall improved from 0.273723 to
0.613172, a delta of +0.339449, which passes the +0.20 coverage gate.

This is a bounded negative with a successful component repair. Native-scale
tiling solved a material portion of the proposal-coverage problem. The remaining
constraint is ranking: the repaired route converted only 29.9% of its oracle
coverage into recall at the frozen false-positive budget, versus 41.5% for the
full-frame baseline.

## What the result resolves

- **Fact:** execution, retrieval, chronology and data-lineage gates passed.
- **Fact:** the frozen primary gate failed; S01-E002 cannot authorise SAM2.
- **Fact:** coverage improved substantially and passed its frozen component
  gate, including large gains at locations 577 and 956.
- **Fact:** high-scoring false positives include road markings and repaired road
  texture, while some IoU-qualified crack candidates sit just below the global
  score threshold.
- **Fact:** validation training peaked at epoch 23, then became unstable from
  epoch 25 and included non-finite validation losses; the frozen `best.pt`
  retained the earlier checkpoint. This does not invalidate the evaluated
  artifact, but it blocks treating the final training trajectory as stable.
- **Inference:** the smallest useful next intervention is tile-native ranking,
  rather than another proposer-scale or segmentation experiment.
- **Uncertainty:** this run does not distinguish reranker domain shift from
  score calibration, location mix, or irreducible visual ambiguity. The current
  confirmatory locations are consumed and cannot be used to choose among them.

## Disposition

Preserve the v1 verdict and close this experiment. Retain the tiled proposer as
a qualified coverage component. Do not tune the score, reranker or threshold on
locations 75, 220, 577, 956 or 1139. Advance only a new, reviewed experiment
that freezes the tiled proposer, trains a tile-native hard-negative ranker on
development data, and uses unused reserve locations for confirmation.

The next experiment should avoid another all-or-nothing interpretation. Its one
claim gate should target the ranking bottleneck directly; component results,
uncertainty and operational value should be reported separately. A practical
draft criterion is at least +0.05 absolute macro-location recall over the frozen
S01-E002 route at the same 1 FP/image budget. This corresponds to recovering at
least one additional crack per 20 targets at fixed review burden. It must be
reviewed and frozen before new evidence is accessed.
