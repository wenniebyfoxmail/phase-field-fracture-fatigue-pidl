## Interpretation and limitation

At this snapshot, V1 and FEM have broadly similar large-scale magnitude, and
the residual panel is visually small over most of the admitted domain. Strong
localisation and a few high-amplitude cells dominate the colour range, so the
mostly dark/white appearance must not be read as pointwise spatial closure.
The scalar R/G table, not this one snapshot, decides the frozen experiment.

Damage and history are identical between V0 and V1; this figure only concerns
the physical-raw channel. It does not establish temporal irreversibility,
energy balance, phase-field convergence, mechanism fidelity or fresh-case
generalisation.

Visual QA: all four panels open correctly; axes and colourbars are legible;
coordinate orientation is consistent; the three compared fields use a shared
scale and the signed residual is zero-centred.
