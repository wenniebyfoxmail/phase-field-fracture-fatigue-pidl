## Findings that change the decision

1. Existing E011 original-Q4 data offer a legitimate same-future-load **candidate search**: 120 cycles × 5 substeps, one repeated loading schedule, complete 600 snapshot inventory. Different cycles at the same phase therefore have different recorded exposure histories and equal future prescribed loading windows. They are one physical/numerical trajectory, not independent specimens. Scalar proximity does not establish equal spatial geometry or a causal history effect.
2. Existing Hard5 U=.11/.12/.13 trajectories do not give a controlled cross-trajectory pair: their future amplitudes differ. Existing MCS endpoints have frozen, different antecedents and zero history commits; late peaks are not all accepted. They cannot be concatenated into new reachable trajectories.
3. New hybrid code has a sufficient explicit restart-state representation and atomic commitment, but its current producer repeats one constant-amplitude five-substep cycle from common state0. Arbitrary history schedules, a lawful branch/restart runner, and matched-origin comparison semantics are not implemented in that producer.
4. A scientifically useful synthetic pair must be generated by two reachable histories and then a common probe and future schedule; do not manually replace A/r/f/H. The generator's own memory law makes the possibility of history dependence unsurprising. The useful question is whether a deliberately limited observable representation loses predictive information, and whether recoverable history restores it.

## Smallest lawful paired-history construction (design, not run authority)

Keep mesh/material/initial crack identical and begin from a common admissible state. Use two **prescribed reachable preconditioning histories** with equal numbers of cycles but different amplitudes/exposure, then exactly the same observation probe phase and future suffix. The target is similar current observable geometry/response with materially different hidden A, not numerically identical full state. Select origins only using prefix/current observations and predeclared uncertainty-scaled matching tolerances. Do not search for pairs by future divergence. Keep unmatched pairs and bounded search exhaustion in the outcome.

A useful mechanism-led candidate is subcritical preconditioning: low vs moderately higher repeated cycles, both before substantial geometry change, then a common small probe. In the fatigue law, A can accumulate while f remains1; after a common future suffix one arm may reach fatigue activation sooner. Whether the existing AT1 model actually supplies the desired overlap and reachable active fatigue segment must be measured, not assumed. Do not hand-set A or freeze d artificially to manufacture the contrast. A different probe itself advances history; save it as actual accepted steps in both arms.

**Subtle null:** if d is fixed, every cycle returns r to0, and f stays1, reordering the same multiset of amplitudes can yield the same summed A. Merely swapping low/high order is not guaranteed to create a hidden-state contrast. It can instead supply a useful null control, subject to actual state verification. A physical fatigue-off null must be specified from the start as a different model, not accomplished by resetting A/f midway. Cheaper predictive nulls are history omission and donor/permuted history conditioned on current geometry, phase, prescribed future and exposure; these are input ablations, never modified FEM initial states.

Scoring should compare current-observation+known exposure/future forcing, then plus usable response history, and full-state oracle. Group by parent/history design and use entirely separate parent pairs for evaluation; thousands of spatial nodes or multiple origin pairs from one path do not supply independent physical units. A one-parent matched pair can falsify geometric-state sufficiency within this generator, not estimate robust generalization.

A toy that merely implements A accumulation and displays two futures repeats an assumed model property and will not by itself change the road-prediction route. It must remain synthetic mechanism/identifiability evidence, not physical validation.
